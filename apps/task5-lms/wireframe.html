1 · Sitemap by role

Learner

  • Dashboard (default page)
  • Home: course catalogue
  • Course page → activity player
  • Learning history & certificates
  • Profile & settings
  • Notifications

Manager

Everything a learner has, plus:

  • My team: progress, overdue, hours
  • Team member detail
  • Assign course with due date
  • Send reminder
  • Export team progress

Administrator

Everything a learner has, plus Site administration:

  • Courses: create, edit content, publish, launch, duplicate, delete
  • Users: add, edit, delete, suspend, roles, managers, CSV upload, bulk actions, log in as
  • Reports: overview, course completion, activity completion, satisfaction, logs, xAPI

Global on every page: language switch (English default / Arabic RTL), light / dark mode, notifications, user menu.
